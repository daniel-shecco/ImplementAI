FAQ

Questions we hear a lot

We're a small business. Is AI really worth it for us?

Often yes — but not always. Small teams usually benefit most, because every hour saved goes straight back to guests or patients. The free Quick-Check exists precisely to give you an honest answer before you spend anything.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

No. We work with the reservation system, PMS, POS or practice software you already use and connect AI to it. Replacing tools is only recommended if it clearly pays off.

What about data protection and patient data?

We design every solution GDPR-first: EU hosting, data processing agreements, minimal data use and clear access rules. For clinics, health data is kept out of general-purpose AI tools unless a compliant setup is in place.

Will AI replace my staff?

That's not our goal. We automate repetitive tasks — the phone calls, the copy-paste, the spreadsheets — so your people can focus on hospitality and care.

How long until we see results?

A first use case is typically live within 4–8 weeks of the audit. We agree on KPIs upfront so you can see the impact in numbers.
