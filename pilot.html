Describe your use case ↗
Is it really free?

Yes. The Starter Pack normally costs €3,900; pilot clients pay nothing for our work. The only possible cost is a tool licence, which you'd pay to the vendor directly, and we tell you the price before anything is set up.

Why are you doing this?

We're a new consultancy. We want real results from real businesses to show future clients, and honest feedback to improve how we work.

What makes an application stand out?

A specific problem that happens often and costs real time or money, described in plain words. You don't need to know which tool could solve it; that's our job.

What if I'm not selected?

We reply to everyone. If your problem is a good fit for AI, you can still book a free one-hour Quick-Check with us.

Will I depend on you afterwards?

No. Every tool and account is in your name, and the training and playbook are designed so your team can run everything alone.

Is my data safe?

We only use your application to assess it. Every setup is GDPR-first, and patient data is kept out of general-purpose AI tools.
