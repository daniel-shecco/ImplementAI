When do you start?

We're preparing to launch in Vienna in 2027 and are not taking on work yet. Registering now puts you first in line for the 10 free places. We'll contact you before we start.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Discovery Pack will cost €1,490; the first 10 businesses pay nothing, and there's no obligation afterwards.

Why are you doing this?

We're three AI and data specialists preparing to start a consultancy. Before we launch, we want to understand which problems matter most to businesses like yours, and the first 10 projects will become our first case studies.

What makes a registration stand out?

A specific problem that happens often and costs real time or money, described in plain words. You don't need to know which tool could solve it; that's our job.

What if I'm not selected?

We reply to everyone. If your problem is a good fit for AI, you'll be able to book a free one-hour Quick-Check once we've launched.

Who is behind ImplementAI?

Daniel Sheccoury (MSc, University of Oxford), Dr. Tibor Sekera (PhD in Physics, AI agents) and Jean Christophe Loeb (senior data scientist, data and forecasting), based in Vienna. You'll meet us in person on the call and on the on-site day.

What happens after the Discovery?

You keep the report and decide. You can implement the recommendations yourselves, or ask us to set them up and train your team as a regular Starter or Complete package. No obligation either way.

Is my data safe?

We only use your registration to contact you about the launch and to choose the 10. If we don't launch, we delete it. Every setup is GDPR-first, and patient data is kept out of general-purpose AI tools.
