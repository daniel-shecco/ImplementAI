Replacing the "would you pay" question

Instead of asking about money, we offer something extra. Whoever takes it is telling us they're seriously interested, without us ever showing a price in the form. Each option replaces the "After the free analysis" block.

AFounding-client perk

A free add-on people choose to claim. Ticking it costs nothing and feels like getting a bonus. Only people who picture working with us afterwards bother to.

A thank-you for being early
  • First in line when we start
  • A free follow-up visit 3 months after the analysis
  • Founding-client rate if you later want help putting the plan into practice
Optional and free. It doesn't commit you to anything.
What it tells us: share of registrations claiming the perk = share open to working with us beyond the free part.

BHow soon?

Urgency is the best predictor of buying. Asking when they want the problem solved feels like we're planning around them, not selling to them.
