Visiting this website

When you open this website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA) automatically processes the technical data your browser sends: IP address, date and time, the page requested, and browser and operating system. This is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We do not keep these log files ourselves; Cloudflare keeps them only as long as needed for delivery and security.

The fonts are loaded from our own server, not from Google. We use no cookies, no analytics tools and no advertising pixels (such as the Meta Pixel).

If you reach the website through one of our ads, the link contains the name of the campaign (for example “utm_campaign=pilot”). If you then send a form, we store this campaign name with it so we know which ad worked. It does not identify you.

Forms: registering your interest and contacting us

When you send a form, we receive the details you enter: name, business, email, phone (optional), industry, team size, the software you use, your description of the problem, how soon you would like it solved and whether you want the founding-client benefits. We also store the date, the page and the language of the form.

We use this to reply to you, to contact you about our launch and to choose the 10 businesses for the free Discovery Pack. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). You then receive a confirmation email from us.

If you tick the case study box, we may publish the findings of your Discovery as a case study, anonymously if you wish. This is based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by email, with effect for the future.

Giving us these details is voluntary, but without them we cannot answer your request. Please do not include health information about your clients or patients in the form.

Registering through Facebook or Instagram

If you register through a form inside Facebook or Instagram, Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. collects your answers on our behalf and passes them to us. We download them from Meta and add them to our registration list. We then process them exactly as described for the website forms. Meta’s own processing when you use Facebook or Instagram is described in Meta’s privacy policy.

Some of these providers or their parent companies are based in the USA. Transfers there are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision under Art. 45 GDPR) or by the EU standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).

How long we keep your data

  • If we decide not to launch: all registrations and enquiries are deleted within 3 months of that decision.
  • Registrations that are not selected: deleted 6 months after the selection date.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to an assignment: deleted 12 months after our last contact.
  • Clients: for the duration of the assignment. Invoices and accounting records are kept for 7 years, as required by Austrian tax law (§ 132 BAO).
  • Server log files: not kept by us (see “Visiting this website”).

No automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you by automated means (Art. 22 GDPR). Every registration is read and assessed by a person in our team.

Your rights

You can withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). An email to us is enough.
