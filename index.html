06 FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

We've never used AI. Is this for us?

Yes — most of our clients are first-timers. We start with one real problem, explain everything in plain language and only move on when your team feels comfortable.

Which tools do you work with?

Whatever fits best: Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, Grok, Gemini and specialised booking, chat and scheduling tools. We're vendor-neutral, earn no commissions, and every licence is in your name.

Which languages do you work in?

English, German, French and Slovak. Meetings, training sessions and written playbooks can be in any of these — or mixed, if your team is. We can also set up chatbots and message templates that answer your guests and patients in many more languages.

Will we depend on you afterwards?

No — that's the point. You get playbooks, a trained in-house AI champion and admin access to everything. If you ever want help again, it's there by the hour, but you won't need a subscription.

What about data protection and patient data?

We choose business plans and settings that don't train on your data, set clear rules for what may go into AI tools, and keep patient data out of general-purpose AI. Everything is set up GDPR-first.

What if it doesn't work for us?

You're never locked in. Every step has a fixed price, you can stop after Discovery and keep the report, half of the implementation fee is only due once your team is up and running, and we retrain at no cost until your team can do the agreed tasks on their own.

Will AI replace my staff?

That's not the goal. The tools take over repetitive work so your people can focus on hospitality and care.
