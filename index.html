06 FAQ

Questions we hear a lot.

We're a small business. Is AI really worth it?

Often yes — small teams feel every saved hour. But not always, and the free Quick-Check exists to give you an honest answer before you spend anything.

Do we need to replace our software?

No. We connect AI to the reservation system, PMS, POS or practice software you already use, and only recommend switching if it clearly pays off.

What about data protection and patient data?

Every solution is GDPR-first: EU hosting, data processing agreements, minimal data and clear access rules. Health data never goes into general-purpose AI tools without a compliant setup.

Will AI replace my staff?

That's not the goal. We automate the repetitive work so your people can focus on hospitality and care.

How fast will we see results?

A first use case is typically live 4–8 weeks after the audit, with KPIs agreed upfront.
